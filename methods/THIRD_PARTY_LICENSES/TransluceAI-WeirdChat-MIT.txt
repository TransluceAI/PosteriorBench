Applies to the texts taken from WeirdChat: the transcript rubrics of two
behaviors in posteriorbench_methods/generalization/data/rubrics.json, which are
those of data/rubrics.parquet in the dataset Transluce/WeirdChat (revision
3460e41491308601cd68ee377011c7b7a8f0b4b3), and the prompt of the rubric judge
in posteriorbench_methods/generalization/judge.py, which is the template of
weirdchat/judge.py in TransluceAI/WeirdChat (commit
8dc004dc4fa1a37a04c694c8a3e38832a7e77ecb).
